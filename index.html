Improving round by round

On CoffeeMug in simulation, OopsieRecover gets one annotated correction per round and HG-DAgger* gets ten. On 25 held-out scenes with new poses, OopsieRecover reaches 84% after 8 corrections; HG-DAgger* is at 28% after 30.

Simulation

Corrections that hold in new poses and new kitchens

Three RoboCasa tasks, π0.5 fine-tuned on the full RoboCasa dataset, 5–8 corrections per task. L0 repeats the state where corrections were collected, L1 moves the objects, L2 moves them into unseen scenes. Only OopsieRecover keeps working past L0.

One correction, ten new scenes

From a single episode of avoid colliding with the steam handle, the robot was driven at the handle in 10 unseen scenes. The detector fired every time, and steering prevented the collision over 100 steps.

Real robot

Steering a frozen π0.5 on a real robot

DROID hardware with the official π0.5 DROID checkpoint, three to six corrections per task. Pooled over three tasks, OopsieRecover raises task progress from 35% to 61%. HG-DAgger* fine-tunes on the same interventions and does worse than the base policy.
